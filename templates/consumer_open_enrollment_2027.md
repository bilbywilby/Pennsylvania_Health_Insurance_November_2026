# Open enrollment for 2027 coverage: what to do now (Pennsylvania)

*Information, not legal or financial advice. Status date: November 8, 2026. Confirm dates at pennie.com or by calling 1-844-844-8040.*

## Key dates

| Date | What it means |
|---|---|
| November 1, 2026 | Open enrollment began |
| December 15, 2026 | Last day to enroll for coverage that starts January 1, 2027 |
| January 15, 2027 | Open enrollment ends (enrolling December 16 or later starts coverage February 1) |

Sources: [Pennsylvania Office of Rural Health](https://www.porh.psu.edu/pa-health-insurance-exchange-2027-open-enrollment-period-returns-to-november-1-2026-january-15-2027/), [PHLP](https://www.phlp.org/en/news/pennie-open-enrollment-what-s-changing-for-2027-coverage), [WHYY](https://whyy.org/articles/affordable-care-act-health-insurance-open-enrollment/).

## Five steps

1. **Log in to Pennie.** Do not assume your plan will roll over unchanged (PHLP).
2. **Update your income and household information** and confirm your address, phone and email so you get notices. If you did not allow Pennie to check your income with tax data, plans may show at full price; contact Pennie or resubmit your application.
3. **Compare plans, even if you like yours.** The Pennsylvania Insurance Department approved an average individual-market increase of 15.97% for 2027, but your price depends on your age, county, plan and subsidy, and some carriers' approved increases are far below the average (for example 1.4% and 8.6%) while others are higher (for example 35.1%). Source: [PID release, September 18, 2026](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/insurance/newsroom/pennsylvania-releases-aca-2027-health-insurance-rates).
4. **Check the yearly cost cap.** For 2027, the most a marketplace plan can require you to pay in a year for covered in-network essential benefits is $12,000 for one person and $24,000 for a family. Many plans set lower caps, and cost-sharing reductions on Silver plans lower them further. Source: [healthinsurance.org](https://www.healthinsurance.org/glossary/out-of-pocket-maximum-limit/) (confirm against the CMS guidance).
5. **Get free help.** Pennie: 1-844-844-8040 (certified assisters and brokers at pennie.com/connect). Pennsylvania Health Law Project helpline: 1-800-274-3258 (if you lost Medicaid or think a decision was wrong).

## If your situation changed

- **Immigration status:** From January 1, 2027, premium tax credits and cost-sharing reductions are limited to green card holders, Cuban/Haitian entrants and COFA migrants. Others may still buy a plan at full price. Ask a Pennie assister before deciding. (PHLP, 2026)
- **Losing Medicaid:** Losing other coverage opens a 60-day special enrollment period. Do not wait out the 60 days. (PHLP, 2026)
- **Income changes during the year:** Report them. You may have to repay tax credits you were not entitled to. (PHLP, 2026)

## Problems with your insurer

File a complaint with the Pennsylvania Insurance Department: [pa.gov/consumer-complaints](https://www.pa.gov/consumer-complaints) or 1-866-PA-COMPLAINT (1-866-722-6675).
